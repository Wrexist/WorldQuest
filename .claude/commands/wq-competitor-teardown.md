Produce a competitor teardown of: **$ARGUMENTS**

Invoke the `worldquest-competitor-teardown` skill.

Answer the five standard questions, and produce every required section: positioning ·
onboarding (timed, step by step) · core loop · retention mechanics · monetisation ·
accessibility check · top 1★ and 5★ reviews · the one thing to steal · the one thing
to avoid · where WorldQuest wins.

**Accuracy rules — these matter more than the analysis:**

- Never state a price, user count or revenue figure you haven't verified today. Mark it
  `TODO(verify)` with the date checked.
- Distinguish observation from inference, and label the inference.
- Cite second-hand claims as second-hand.

An unverified number that escapes into a pitch deck is worse than no number.

Finish by updating the gap table at the end of
`docs/product/competitive-research.md`: does this product close one of our five gaps,
or open a new one? A teardown that doesn't change the synthesis isn't finished.
