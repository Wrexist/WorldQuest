Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished 3D GAME ASSET CONCEPT MOCKUP for WorldQuest, a cheerful geography-learning mobile game. This is a design preview before producing the actual 3D model. The existing mascot is a friendly round blue-and-green Earth explorer with a khaki safari hat, a round blue globe badge, a small tan backpack and chunky mustard boots. The app has pale ice-blue backgrounds, deep navy rounded cards, warm yellow rewards, lime-green completed milestones and friendly rounded Nunito-like lettering.

Main concept: ATLAS'S EXPLORER TRUNK. Design an original, lovable, premium little treasure trunk which feels like it belongs in that explorer's equipment. Give it a distinctive squat, slightly wider-than-tall silhouette with generously soft toy-like corners, a substantial rounded domed hinged lid, warm honey-tan shell and broad caramel leather bands. Ocean-blue enamel inset panels, chunky pale golden rounded corner protectors, short rounded feet. ONE large iconic circular ocean-blue globe medallion in a warm brass rim as the central latch: simple raised latitude/longitude arcs, instantly legible at 96 pixels. No face or eyes on the chest. A clear dark seam separates the domed lid from the body, rear hinge practical for opening animation. Materials: smooth satin toy/clay, soft leather-like bands, friendly tactile molded forms; restrained broad highlights, crisp modeled edges, excellent form shading. Make the trunk feel touchable, playful and full of anticipation, not an ordinary box. The TAN silhouette must dominate: do not make another mostly blue chest with straight thin yellow strips.

Composition: a beautiful landscape concept sheet on a clean pale ice-blue studio background. Upper left has the short dark-navy title 'Explorer's treasure'. Feature a LARGE three-quarter CLOSED chest on the left with all of its silhouette visible, soft contact shadow and generous space. On the upper right show the SAME chest OPEN, same geometry/materials/straps/latch, lid hinged back; a few chunky gold XP medallions and a simple folded ivory map with an abstract dotted route rest inside, one gold star hovering just above the opening. No purple gems. Keep rewards modest and intentional, no explosion of coins or magical glow. At lower right include ONE elegant small navy rounded mobile UI card to show the proposed chest at actual interface scale: warm gold '+50 XP', white 'Today's treasure', five simple connected circular milestones below, and the SAME closed chest on the right. This inset is a concept, not a screenshot of a shipped product. Rounded friendly typography. The two hero views are the focus, not the interface. Labels beneath the hero renders: 'Ready to explore' and 'Quest complete'. No other labels, annotations, technical measurements or palettes. High-end coherent mobile-game art direction. Consistent 65mm three-quarter view slightly from above, big soft area light from upper left, gentle ambient occlusion, no gritty textures, tiny buckles, realistic weathering, hard metallic reflections, glow halos or ornate pirate decorations. Not photoreal, not flat vector, not low-poly. Clean art suitable to be faithfully modeled and animated in Blender later.
