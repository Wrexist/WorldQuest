Reviewed the two supplied videos through their public media attachments, including sampled frames, and read the linked 3dicon workflow. This is an implementation assessment, not a claim that the proposed assets have been produced.

Its distinction between self-moving objects, functional actions, moving parts, and surface changes is useful for our asset briefs. ## Applied now: direct answers

Lesson answer choices commit on activation and show the existing verdict immediately. Check is removed from multiple-choice questions; Continue still controls the next question. Typed input retains explicit submission and matching boards retain their own completion behavior. This serves quick practice without adding animation latency. The reducer's existing answered phase rejects subsequent taps, and grading/economy logic is unchanged.

## Production constraints

Use WorldQuest's approved clay Atlas, materials, lighting and semantic colors. Generate new art only where the current asset cannot express its intended action. Do not import the reference artwork or add leaderboards/social sign-in from the example to our onboarding.

Prototype the streak flame first. Validate its silhouette at actual display size, transparency in both themes, playback on iPhone, peak decode memory, offscreen/background suspension, and a static reduced-motion fallback. Avoid a continuously animated header or multiple simultaneous reward loops: the app has an existing stutter report and device performance is an acceptance criterion.

No external generation service was installed or invoked, and no reference asset was bundled into the application.
