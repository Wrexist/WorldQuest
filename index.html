How it works

A lesson in three moves.

No lectures and no walls of text. You meet a few new facts, answer questions about them, and the app decides when you'll see each one again.

1

Meet it

New countries arrive with their map, so the shape comes before the quiz.

2

Answer it

Pick, then check. A wrong answer shows you the right one and moves on. No buzzer.

3

Keep it

Each fact is scheduled to come back right before you'd forget it. That's what makes it stick.

Why it works

Built on how memory works.

Not a trivia game. WorldQuest keeps track of every fact you've met and brings each one back at the right moment.

A daily plan, made for you

Each day mixes new countries with the ones you're starting to forget. Five challenges, about ten minutes, and then you're done. It never becomes an endless feed.

Mistakes are the lesson

Missed questions come back once at the end of the lesson as practice. No hearts lost, no shame.

Streaks that forgive

A freeze covers a missed day, and after a break you can often repair the streak. We'd rather you came back.

Facts you can trust

Every fact carries a source and the date it was checked, and the values are cross-checked against an independent dataset.

Offline by design

Finish a lesson on a plane or in a tunnel. Progress waits on your phone and syncs when you're back.

English and Swedish

The whole app, every question and every country name, in either language. Switch any time in Settings.

For parents

Safe for curious kids.

Children's protections are built into the app and our server, not left as a setting to remember.

  • No ads and no tracking, for anyone
  • No email asked for on a child's device
  • No leaderboards, chat or strangers
  • A grown-up question before any link out
  • No purchases at all
  • Delete everything any time, in the app
