Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

WorldQuest is a learning app used by children as well as adults. We treat privacy as a product feature rather than a legal formality, and this policy is written to be readable rather than defensive. If anything here is unclear, ask us — the contact address is at the bottom.

The short version. We collect the minimum needed to save your progress and run a subscription. We never sell your data, never show ads, and never collect advertising identifiers on any account. Children's accounts collect dramatically less than adult ones, by design and not by setting.

We store your birth year, not your date of birth. That is enough to apply the right protections and less than we could ask for.

2. What we never collect

Not now, not later, not on any account.

  • Advertising identifiers — we run no ads and collect no ad IDs
  • Location — of any kind, precise or approximate
  • Your contacts
  • Photos, camera, microphone — the app never asks, and on Android the microphone permission is explicitly blocked
  • Browsing or search history outside the app
  • Health, financial or biometric data

We also never receive your card details. Payments are handled entirely by Apple and Google; we are told only whether a subscription is active.

3. Children

WorldQuest is designed to be safe for children, and the protections below are enforced in code — not offered as a setting someone can forget to switch on.

An account is marked as a child account at sign-up, from the age you enter. That marking is permanent and cannot be edited afterwards, so a child cannot age out of protection by changing a field.

On a child account

  • No third-party analytics. The analytics layer does nothing at all.
  • No email address, no real name, no photo, no location is collected.
  • No purchases. Purchase screens are hidden entirely, not merely disabled. Premium reaches a child only through a parent's plan.
  • No social features — they are absent, not switched off.
  • Notifications are limited to one per day and never after 7pm local time.

Where the law requires it, we ask for verifiable parental consent before creating a child's account, and we record that consent. A parent can view their child's progress, export their data, or delete the account at any time.

6. How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete it, the account is deactivated immediately and permanently purged after 30 days. The 30-day window exists so an accidental deletion can be undone — not so we can keep your data.

7. Security

Every piece of your data is protected by database-level access rules that make it unreadable to any other account. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Data on your device is encrypted at rest, and sign-in tokens are held in the operating system's secure keychain. Our logs never contain passwords, tokens, or anything you answered in a lesson.

Nobody can read your raw answers but you. Not teachers, not parents, not our support staff, not our administrators. That is a limit built into the system's design, not a policy someone can override.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect — not quietly update a page and hope you re-read it.

9. Contact

Questions, data requests, or anything you think we have got wrong: isacmolin@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days, and to data requests within 30 days.
