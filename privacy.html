Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · applies to WorldQuest 1.0 for iPhone

WorldQuest is a geography learning app used by children as well as adults. This page says plainly what the app collects, why, and how to remove it. If anything is unclear, write to us; the address is at the bottom.

The short version. You can play without an account. We keep your learning progress so it survives a lost phone, and your email only if you choose to link one. No ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no location, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

1. What we collect

The app asks for your birth year during setup. It is kept on your phone. Our server receives it once to work out your age group and stores only the group, not the year.

Settings such as your language, reminder time and avatar stay on your phone.

2. What we never collect

  • Advertising identifiers, or any data for advertising
  • Location, precise or approximate
  • Contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Your name (you are never asked for it)
  • Browsing history, health, financial or biometric data

WorldQuest 1.0 contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. If Apple shows us a crash report, it comes from Apple's own opt-in diagnostics, under Apple's policy.

WorldQuest 1.0 sells nothing, so it handles no payments.

3. Children

Children's protections are built into the app, not left as a setting:

  • A child's device is never asked for an email address.
  • Leaderboards, friends and anything social are off.
  • Every link out of the app, and account deletion, first asks a grown-up a question a young child is unlikely to answer.
  • Our server treats the account as a child's too, and an age group can move down to "child" but never back up.
  • Reminders are local to the phone and limited to one a day.

A parent or guardian can delete a child's progress in the app (Settings › Privacy & data, behind the grown-up question) or by writing to us.

5. Why we are allowed to (GDPR)

We process this data to provide the app you asked to use (contract). We do not use consent-based tracking because we do not track.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Sign-in codes expire within minutes.

Deleting your account (Settings › Privacy & data › Delete account) erases your account, progress, email and reports from our live database straight away. If you linked an email, you confirm with a code first. Cloudflare's database backups roll off within 30 days. Deleting the app without deleting the account leaves the account on our server; write to us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete what we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us; we answer data requests within 30 days. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted. Your sign-in token is kept in the phone's secure keychain and stored on our side only as a hash. Each account can read only its own data, and the server, not the app, decides rewards.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect, and update the date above.

10. Contact

WorldQuest is made by an independent developer, who is responsible for this data. Questions, data requests, or anything you think we got wrong: isacmolin@gmail.com.
