Support

A person reads every message, usually within a couple of days.

Write to us about anything: a bug, a wrong fact, your account, your data.

isacmolin@gmail.com

It helps to include your iPhone model, the app version (Settings › About), and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

I missed a day. Is my streak gone?

Not necessarily. A streak freeze, if you hold one, covers a missed day on its own. After a break the app may also offer a streak repair for a short time. You can get freezes in the Shop with coins.

I finished a lesson offline. Where is my XP?

It is saved on your phone and sent the next time you are online. Open the app with a connection and it catches up by itself. Nothing is lost in a tunnel.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Link an email first: Settings › Account › Link your email. On the new phone, open Settings › Account › I already have an account and enter the code we email you. Without a linked email, progress stays with the old phone.

The sign-in code did not arrive.

Check spam and wait a minute; you can ask for a new code after 60 seconds. Codes last five minutes. Still nothing? Write to us from that address.

I think a fact is wrong.

Please tell us. After answering, tap Report a problem on the answer sheet and pick a reason, or write to us with the question and what you believe is right. A wrong fact is a real bug to us.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Privacy & data › Delete account. It erases your progress from our server straight away; if you linked an email you confirm with a code first. On a child's device a grown-up question comes first. You can also write to us. Details are in the privacy policy.

My child uses WorldQuest. What should I know?

On a child's device there is no email sign-up, no leaderboards or social features, no purchases, and no ads or tracking for anyone. Links out of the app ask a grown-up first. You can delete your child's progress at any time.

Does WorldQuest cost anything?

No. WorldQuest 1.0 is free and has no in-app purchases or ads.
