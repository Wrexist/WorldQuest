Common questions

I lost my streak. Can I get it back?

Usually, yes — open the app and you should be offered a streak repair. We would rather you kept learning than felt punished for a busy day.

If the repair isn't offered and you think it should have been, email us with your account email and roughly when the streak broke.

My progress is missing.

First, check you are signed into the same account — progress follows the account, not the device.

If you were playing as a guest and then signed in, your guest progress should have carried over. If it didn't, email us before playing much more and include the approximate date you signed up. We keep a full history of every lesson and can usually restore it.

I found a fact that's wrong.

Please tell us. Every fact in WorldQuest is meant to be sourced and dated, and a wrong one is a real bug, not a rounding error.

Send the question as it appeared and what you believe the right answer is. Content fixes ship without an app update, so it can usually be corrected the same day.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so they are cancelled there:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → WorldQuest → Cancel
  • Android: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling never deletes your progress — lessons, streaks, XP and everything you've learned stay exactly as they are. Only Premium's extras pause.

I was charged after cancelling / I want a refund.

Refunds are handled by the store that took the payment, not by us:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google: Play Store → Order history → Report a problem

Tell us as well and we'll back up your request where we can.

My payment failed but I didn't cancel.

This is usually an expired card or a bank declining the charge, and it's more common than people expect. Update your payment method in your store account and access resumes automatically — there's nothing to buy again.

Your progress is never affected by a billing problem.

How do I delete my account or get a copy of my data?

Both are in Settings inside the app.

Deleting deactivates the account straight away and permanently erases it after 30 days — that window exists so an accidental deletion can be undone. An export gives you everything we hold in a machine-readable file.

You can also just email us and we'll do it for you. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

My child uses WorldQuest. What should I know?

Child accounts are treated differently in code, not by a setting:

  • No third-party analytics at all
  • No email address, real name, photo or location is collected
  • No purchase screens — they're hidden entirely, not just disabled
  • No social features, no chat, no strangers
  • At most one notification per day, never after 7pm

You can request your child's data or have the account deleted at any time.

Does WorldQuest work offline?

Yes. You can start and finish a lesson with no connection at all — it was built for the commute. Your progress is queued on the device and syncs the next time you're online. Nothing is lost in a tunnel.
